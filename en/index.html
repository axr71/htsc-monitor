Experimental · New · November 2026 · arXiv:2611.01118

Direct imaging of the Ni eg hole orbitals in the bilayer nickelate La₂SmNi₂O₇

Ethan Saïman and colleagues use non-resonant inelastic X-ray scattering at the Ni M1 edge to map 3d hole orbital anisotropy in La2SmNi2O7. The measured orbital-hole ratio is 0.94 ± 0.06, supporting scenarios in which both eg orbitals matter for pairing.

Emerging trends archive

Interpretive timeline reconstructed from weekly monitoring reports in October–November 2026. Dates refer to reporting periods, not discovery dates. These trends are research syntheses, not settled conclusions.

26 ottobre – 2 novembre 2026 · Fononi · cuprati

The B₁g phonon returns to the center of d-wave pairing

Two independent computational studies using Hubbard and three-band models suggest that oxygen B₁g phonon fluctuations can cooperate with electronic correlations to strengthen d-wave pairing. The convergence is promising but model-dependent and not direct experimental proof of a universal mechanism.

Related papers: Hubbard + fonone B₁g · Modello a tre bande

19–26 ottobre 2026 · Cuprati · pseudogap

Magnetic fluctuations and competition with the pseudogap

Two experimental Nature Physics studies offer complementary perspectives: anisotropic scattering tracks the critical temperature in electron-doped cuprates, while ultrafast optical suppression of superconductivity reveals a pseudogap-related response. These results concern different materials and experimental protocols.

Related papers: Scattering e pairing · Pseudogap indotto dalla luce

12–19 ottobre 2026 · Nickelati · multistrato

Multilayer nickelates and orbital physics

Recent reports highlight nickelates with multiple NiO₂ layers and studies comparing electronic structure, magnetic excitations and pairing symmetry. Their relationship to cuprates remains unresolved.

Related papers: Nickelato a cinque strati · Dinamica magnetica nei cuprati

5–12 ottobre 2026 · Ordini elettronici · materiali

Electronic orders, textures and material discovery

Alongside electronic modulation studies in cuprates, theoretical nickelate models and predictive hydride studies underscore the need to distinguish charge ordering from the mechanisms stabilizing superconductivity.

Related papers: Modulazioni elettroniche · Idruri: approccio predittivo
